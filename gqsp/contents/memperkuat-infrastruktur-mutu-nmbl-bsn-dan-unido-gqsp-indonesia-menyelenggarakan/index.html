Strengthening Quality Infrastructure: NMBL and UNIDO-GQSP Indonesia Conduct Internal Audit Training for ISO/IEC 17025 Implementation

Serpong, GQSP Indonesia (15/05) - Internal audits are a fundamental of ensuring compliance with the ISO/IEC 17025 standard, a key requirement for laboratories seeking accreditation from Indonesia's National Accreditation Committee (KAN). However, the National Metrology Biology Laboratory (NMBL) faced a significant challenge—the staffs that had the necessary experience or knowledge to conduct internal audits are very limited. 

In response, UNIDO’s Global Quality and Standards Programme (GQSP) Indonesia, in collaboration with the National Standardization Agency (BSN) conducted a three-day intensive training on internal auditing for ISO/IEC 17025 from May 6 to 8, 2024. The aim was clear: equip the NMBL personnel with the skills needed to independently conduct internal audits and ensure their laboratory’s compliance to internationally recognized standards. Five staff members from NMBL participated in this transformative training, which was led by two esteemed experts, Mr. Sunarya and Mrs. Murtiningsih. On the first day, Mr. Sunarya provided foundational insights into the internal audit process, while on the following two days, both trainers guided participants through hands-on audit exercises, simulating real scenarios to reinforce learning. This effort is part of a broader strategy to strengthen Indonesia's quality infrastructure, particularly in sectors vital to the country's economy, like fisheries. By enhancing the audit capacities of NMBL, the training contributes to ensuring that the laboratory continues to meet KAN's accreditation requirements, ultimately improving the quality and safety of fishery products across Indonesia.

A special note of appreciation goes to Mr. Sunarya and Mrs. Murtiningsih for their invaluable expertise and guidance throughout the training. Their contributions not only equipped NMBL staff with critical auditing skills but also reinforced Indonesia’s commitment to maintaining high standards in its laboratory practices.

GQSP Indonesia Phase 2 strengthens quality and standards in Indonesia’s fisheries value chains.
