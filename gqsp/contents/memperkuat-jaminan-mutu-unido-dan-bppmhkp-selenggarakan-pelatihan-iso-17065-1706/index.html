Strengthening Quality Assurance: UNIDO and BPPMHKP Conduct Training on ISO 17065, 17067, and 17026 for Marine and Fisheries Personnel

Jakarta, April 22–23, 2025 — As part of ongoing efforts to enhance the credibility of Indonesia’s quality infrastructure in the marine and fisheries sector, the Global Quality and Standards Programme (GQSP) Indonesia—implemented by UNIDO—collaborated with the Marine and Fisheries Quality Assurance Agency (BPPMHKP), Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries, to conduct a two-day training on ISO 17065, ISO 17067, and ISO 17026.

The training, held at the BUSPM facility in Setu, Jakarta, brought together a total of 743 government officers from across Indonesia, comprising 45 participants onsite and 698 participants online. The sessions were led by Dr. Sunarya, Quality Infrastructure Expert from UNIDO GQSP Indonesia, who shared in-depth knowledge on the application and importance of these international standards.

These standards—ISO/IEC 17065 (certification of products, processes, and services), ISO/IEC 17067 (fundamentals of product certification), and ISO/IEC 17026 (evaluation of conformity assessment schemes)—play a pivotal role in strengthening the national certification and accreditation system. The training aimed to equip participants with the technical understanding and practical know-how required to ensure conformity with international requirements, support effective regulatory oversight, and facilitate fair trade in marine and fisheries products.

Participants gained insights into the structure and key principles of the standards, including impartiality and competence requirements, the development and operation of certification schemes, and best practices for regulatory surveillance. The interactive format encouraged knowledge exchange among government officials, many of whom are directly involved in market surveillance, inspection, and certification processes at regional levels.

This training forms part of a broader collaboration between UNIDO GQSP Indonesia and BPPMHKP to strengthen institutional capacity in quality assurance and regulatory compliance. Through this capacity building efforts, the programme aims to ensure that Indonesian marine and fisheries products not only meet domestic standards but are also competitive and trusted in global markets.

GQSP Indonesia Phase 2 strengthens quality and standards in Indonesia’s fisheries value chains.
