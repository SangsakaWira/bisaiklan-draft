Indonesia Shrimp Industry Roundtable

The United States Soybean Export Council (USSEC) hosted the Indonesia Shrimp Industry Roundtable once again, gathering stakeholders from across Indonesia’s shrimp value chain to address production challenges and improve the industry's competitiveness. Held in Jakarta on Monday, September 30, 2024, the event provided a forum to explore pressing issues and potential solutions. Pamudi from USSEC highlighted the urgency of this inaugural roundtable, noting the critical internal and external challenges currently facing Indonesia's shrimp industry.

The event featured two key speakers: Haris Muhtadi from Shrimp Club Indonesia and Aris Utama, a representative of the Global Shrimp Council (GSC). Both speakers highlighted the importance of building positive narratives around farmed shrimp. Aris noted that the GSC has launched a global campaign called "Happy Protein" to boost shrimp’s image as a source of protein that supports both physical and mental health. Stakeholders agreed on the need to create and spread positive stories about Indonesian shrimp, emphasizing its nutritional value, social benefits, and environmental impact.

Haris remarked that it’s time for a widespread campaign to enhance the international competitiveness of Indonesian shrimp. "We must build a narrative that highlights not only the quality of Indonesian shrimp but also its production through sustainable, environmentally responsible practices. All stakeholders, from upstream to downstream, must work together more seriously—this is a large, long-term effort,” he said.

Sudari Pawiro, National Chief Technical Advisor (NTCA) at UNIDO Indonesia and an attendee at the event, shared his perspective. “At the USSEC meeting, two key points were  discussed : expanding the shrimp market and promoting and branding Indonesian shrimp. By focusing seriously on local market development, Indonesia’s shrimp industry can achieve more stable growth. Additionally, shrimp promotion and branding are crucial, and we already have a generic brand, ‘Indonesian Shrimp,’ managed by AP5I.”
