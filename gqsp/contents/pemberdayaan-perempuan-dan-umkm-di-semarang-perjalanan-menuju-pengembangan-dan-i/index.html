Empowering Women and SMEs in Semarang: A Journey of Growth and Innovation for Milkfish Value Chain

Semarang , GQSP Indonesia (10/03/2024) - In Semarang, Central Java, a collaborative effort is underway to strengthen the capacity of local milkfish processors. Led by GQSP National Experts Mrs. Novia Priyana and Mr. Mumfaizin, alongside Universitas Negeri Semarang (UNNES) and the  Fisheries and Marine Authorities  (DKP) of Semarang City, the initiative focuses on improving the technical capacities of SMEs and MSMEs in the milkfish processing industry.

The project's main objective is to help these businesses meet food safety regulations, buyer standards, and secure certifications such as Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP), Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP), and Indonesia’s National Standard (SNI). This initiative is not only vital for compliance but also contributes to the overall growth of the local economy by improving product quality and competitiveness.

Gap Assessment and Capacity Building

To kick off the project, a thorough three-day (8 - 10 March, 2024) gap assessment was conducted for 10 selected milkfish processing SMEs in Semarang. The assessment provided a baseline for understanding the SMEs' current practices, identifying gaps, and developing targeted capacity-building programs. By focusing on food safety requirements, the project ensures that the SMEs are well-prepared to pursue certifications such as GMP, HACCP, and BPOM Number registration.

Focus on Women Entrepreneurs

Notably, 9 of the 10 selected SMEs are led by women, demonstrating a strong emphasis on empowering women entrepreneurs in the fish processing sector. These women are at the forefront of driving change in their businesses, with support from the project helping them to adopt better practices and reach new markets. By investing in women-led businesses, the initiative strengthens local communities and fosters inclusive economic growth.

UNNES Support and Innovation

As a key partner, UNNES has provided crucial technical support to the milkfish processors. The university has introduced 15 high-pressure cookers to help improve cooking control, ensuring better safety and consistency in the final products. This practical innovation is essential for maintaining food safety and achieving compliance with buyer standards. Moreover, UNNES has involved local quality coaches and students in the project to further enhance the support system for SMEs. This partnership not only benefits the participating businesses but also enriches the students' learning experience, creating a bridge between academic knowledge and industry needs.

Toward Sustainable Growth

This collaboration between GQSP Indonesia, UNNES, and DKP Semarang aims to secure essential certifications for SMEs, empowering them to access broader markets and ensuring the sustainability of their businesses. The project stands as an example of how targeted interventions can uplift small businesses, particularly women-led enterprises, while contributing to the overall economic development of Central Java.

By enhancing food safety standards and improving technical capacities, the initiative supports the long-term success of milkfish processors in the region, ultimately fostering a thriving and sustainable local industry.
