TALKSHOW: Enhancing the Competitiveness of Marine and Fisheries Products through implementing Indonesian National Standards (SNI)

Open document

November is a special month for fisheries people, as we celebrate National Quality Month and National Fish Day this month. 

In this notable momentum, we are proudly present a Talkshow on Peningkatan Daya Saing Produk KP Melalui Penerapan Standar Nasional Indonesia (SNI), organized virtually on:

📅 Date: 20 November 2024
⏰ Time: 09.00 - 12.00 WIB

Register yourself through:

🔗 Link : https://bit.ly/Registrasizoom_Talkshow20112024

You can follow the live-streaming event on:
🔴 Live streaming YouTube Ditjen PDSPKP.

This is a good opportunity to improve understanding of the importance of SNI implementation for fisheries products and procedures for SNI certificate.
