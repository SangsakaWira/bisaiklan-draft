Support the Development of the Indonesian Seaweed Industry

Info Akuakultur (11/2024) - UNIDO GQSP Indonesia remains committed to supporting the development and competitiveness of the national seaweed industry. At the SIAL Interfood event, UNIDO-GQSP Indonesia played an active role by supporting the participation of the Tropical Seaweed Innovation Network (TSIN) and other UNIDO partners in the Indonesia Seaweed Pavilion exhibition, while also engaging in focus group discussions (FGDs) and talk shows. Since 2014, UNIDO, through the SMART-Fish and GQSP Indonesia programs—a collaboration with the Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries (KKP) and funded by the Swiss government through SECO—has implemented various upstream-to-downstream activities across 22 districts and cities in different regions. These activities involved partners such as ASTRULI (the Indonesian Seaweed Association), farmer groups, cooperatives, and seaweed processors. Together with partners and other stakeholders, UNIDO supported the establishment of TSIN, which is now managed under the Directorate General of Strengthening Competitiveness of Marine and Fishery Products (PDSPKP-KKP), the development of six Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for seaweed cultivation, seedling, and preservation (https://gqspindonesia.org/en), the preparation of 15 training modules on seaweed farming, and the creation of the generic brand Indonesia Seaweed managed by ASTRULI.
