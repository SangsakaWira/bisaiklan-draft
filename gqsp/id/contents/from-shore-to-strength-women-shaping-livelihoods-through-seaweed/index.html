From shore to strength: women shaping livelihoods through seaweed

Through the GQSP Indonesia programme, UNIDO has supported sustainable seaweed value chains across Indonesia. Maria Gigih Setiarti has been a driving force, not only training women in seaweed processing but also mentoring them in entrepreneurship including business management and marketing strategies. Her efforts help turn seaweed into a source of viable, sustainable livelihood while empowering coastal communities.

The article below, originally published by UNIDO, showcases how Maria’s leadership is strengthening women-led enterprises and transforming seaweed-based livelihoods.
