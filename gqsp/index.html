About the programme

Quality infrastructure, capable producers and a culture of quality

The Global Quality and Standards Programme (GQSP) is funded by the Swiss State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) and implemented by UNIDO. In Indonesia, Phase 2 works with the Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries (MMAF) and the National Standardization Agency (BSN).
